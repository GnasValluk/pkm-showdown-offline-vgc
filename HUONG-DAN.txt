LOI THUONG GAP
--------------
- Port 8000/8080 bi chiem: tat tab/server cu, hoac doi port:
  set PS_CLIENT_PORT=8090 && node start.js
- Bot ten "Guest": cho 3-5s de bot doi ten thanh OfflineBot.
- Team bao invalid: nho dung Stat Points (VD: 32 HP / 32 Atk / 2 Def = 66),
  IV 31 het, item khong trung (Item Clause), item phai co trong Champions.
- Muon lam .exe: dung cong cu Bat To Exe Converter tro Choi-Ngay.bat -> .exe,
  hoac chay `npm install -g pkg` roi `pkg start.js` (nang cao).
